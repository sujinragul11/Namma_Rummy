NAMMA RUMMY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains what Namma Rummy ("the app"), published by Sujin Ragul, stores about you and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account details you give us: a username, an email address, a mobile number and a password. The password is stored only as a one-way hash; we cannot read it.
  • Game records: the games you played, your seat, your final cards, your score and whether you won. Other players at your table see your username and your play.
  • Play coins: your balance of coins, and the coins you paid in, got back or won at each table. Coins are tokens of the game and have nothing to do with money.
  • Friends: the players you add as friends inside the app and the friend requests you send or receive. This is not your phone's contact list, which the app never reads. Players you have accepted as friends can see whether you are online or in a game.
  • Profile photo, if you add one: the app turns the picture you pick into a small square photo on your phone and sends us only that. The players at your table, your friends and the players you exchange friend requests with see it. You can remove it at any time in Settings.
  • Technical data: like any internet service, the server receives your IP address when the app connects, and it may appear in short-lived server logs.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera or microphone, and it does not read your photo library: it opens only the one picture you pick when you choose a profile photo. It contains no third-party analytics.

Ads

The Android app shows full-screen ads at a few moments: before you sit down at a table, in the break between rounds, and after a game. The ads come from Google AdMob, a service of Google. To choose and count ads, Google's software in the app collects from your device: its advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location can be worked out), information about the device and the app, and how you interact with the ads. Google receives this directly and uses it as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not send Google your account details, your games or your friends list.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads may be personalised, the first time it starts. You can change that choice at any time under Settings > Privacy choices for ads. Anywhere, you can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads on most Android phones).

How we use it

Only to run the game: to log you in, seat you at a table, show your name to the players you play with, keep your results, let you find and invite your friends, and protect the service against abuse. We do not sell your data, and we do not use what is stored on our servers for advertising.

Who receives it

Your account and game data are kept on servers run for us by our hosting provider. We do not share them with anyone else, unless the law requires us to. The ad data described under Ads goes from your device to Google directly.

On your device

The app keeps your login on your device so you stay signed in. Logging out removes it. Your sound settings are kept on the device too and are never sent anywhere.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your account until you delete it. You can delete it at any time, in the app (Profile › Delete account) or on the web at the account deletion page. Deleting removes your username, email address, mobile number, password hash, profile photo, your game results, your coins and their history, and your friends list straight away. Records of the tables themselves stay, without anything that identifies you.

Children

The app is meant for adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it by writing to the support address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

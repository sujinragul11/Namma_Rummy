NAMMA RUMMY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains what Namma Rummy ("the app"), published by Sujin Ragul, stores about you and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account details you give us: a username, an email address, a mobile number and a password. The password is stored only as a one-way hash; we cannot read it.
  • Game records: the games you played, your seat, your final cards, your score and whether you won. Other players at your table see your username and your play.
  • Play coins: your balance of coins, and the coins you paid in, got back or won at each table. Coins are tokens of the game and have nothing to do with money.
  • Friends: the players you add as friends inside the app and the friend requests you send or receive. This is not your phone's contact list, which the app never reads. Players you have accepted as friends can see whether you are online or in a game.
  • Technical data: like any internet service, the server receives your IP address when the app connects, and it may appear in short-lived server logs.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. It contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

How we use it

Only to run the game: to log you in, seat you at a table, show your name to the players you play with, keep your results, let you find and invite your friends, and protect the service against abuse. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who receives it

Your data is kept on servers run for us by our hosting provider. We do not share it with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

On your device

The app keeps your login on your device so you stay signed in. Logging out removes it. Your sound settings are kept on the device too and are never sent anywhere.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your account until you delete it. You can delete it at any time, in the app (Profile › Delete account) or on the web at the account deletion page. Deleting removes your username, email address, mobile number, password hash, your game results, your coins and their history, and your friends list straight away. Records of the tables themselves stay, without anything that identifies you.

Children

The app is meant for adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it by writing to the support address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

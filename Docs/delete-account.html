NAMMA RUMMY

Delete your account

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This page is for players of Namma Rummy, the card game published by Sujin Ragul. If you no longer want your account, you can delete it yourself in the app or on the web in under a minute, or ask us to delete it for you. Deleting an account is permanent.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Namma Rummy and log in.
  2. Tap your name or picture at the top-left of the main menu. Your profile opens.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Type your password and tap Delete.

Your account and its data are deleted at once, and the app returns to the login screen.

Delete it on the web, without the app

If the app is already uninstalled, or you would rather use a browser:

  1. Open the account deletion page on our server with the button below.
  2. Enter your email address, mobile number or username, and your password.
  3. Tap Delete my account.

Open the account deletion page

If you cannot log in

If you have forgotten your password, email sujinragul728@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my account" and your username. We will delete the account and reply to confirm, normally within 7 days.

What is deleted

All of this is removed immediately, in one step, and cannot be restored:

  • your username, email address and mobile number;
  • your password (we only ever store a one-way hash of it, and that hash is deleted);
  • your profile photo, if you added one;
  • your game results: your seat at each table, your final cards, your scores and your wins;
  • your play coins: the balance and the history of what you paid in, got back or won;
  • your friends list and your friend requests, sent and received.

The login kept on your phone stops working as well, so the app cannot sign in to the deleted account again.

What is kept

  • Records of the tables you played at stay, because they also belong to the other players who sat there. Nothing in them identifies you any more: your seat shows no player, and the moves and coin entries from those games are no longer linked to any account.
  • Short-lived server logs may hold your IP address for a short time, as with any internet service. They are not linked to your account.
  • Emails you sent to support stay in our mailbox. Say so in your email if you want them deleted too.

There is no other retention period: the data listed under "What is deleted" is removed the moment you delete the account, not after a waiting time.
